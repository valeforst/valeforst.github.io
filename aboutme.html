About me

I am a recent Master Graduate in Biomedical Engineering, specialized in Biomedical Strumentation, which focused on programming, AI, data analysis and development of embedded systems. I chose this path to combine my passion for helping others with my innate logical and analytical spirit.

My thesis, titled "Analysis of Intraoperative MER Signals in Pediatric Patients with Dystonia Undergoing DBS of the Globus Pallidus Internus", was accepted for publication at IX Congress of the National Group of Bioengineering (GNB), and will be presented in June 2025 in Palermo.

During my academic journey, I worked on AI and data analysis projects using MATLAB, Python (Pandas, scikit-learn) and R, applying these tools to real-world healthcare problems. I also gained hand-on experience with embedded programming (Arduino, ATmega328p) and learned about medical quality standards, like MDR 2017/745, ISO 13485 and many others.
Learn more about my Projects.

I have been part of several international and interdisciplinary teams, thanks to my fluency in English (IELTS C1).

Right now, I am learning web development.

Learning has always been my biggest passion, and my dream is to use my knowledge to make a difference in the world.
